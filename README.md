# Electric-Vehicles-app
Problem Statement: 
*Skills*
Requirements analysis, UI/UX design, mobile app development, web development, database design, REST API development, basic AI/LLM integration, testing and documentation.
*Resources Required*
Student laptops, development tools, database server or cloud hosting, test accounts and sample maintenance data from workshop partner.
*Motivation and Benefits*
Motivation
Many vehicle owners stop returning to authorised dealers after their vehicle warranty expires. Independent workshops can provide good technical servicing, but they often lack a structured digital platform to retain customers, track service history, manage future maintenance, and provide dealer-like reminders. The app aims to help workshops manage customer vehicles proactively through a mobile app, workshop dashboard, automated reminders, wear-and-tear tracking, and an AI maintenance advisor.

Benefits
•	Provides independent workshops with a dealer-like digital service experience for out-of-warranty vehicles.
•	Improves customer retention through automated maintenance reminders, service history visibility, loyalty features and appointment booking.
•	Helps vehicle owners monitor wear-and-tear parts, upcoming service needs and estimated vehicle condition without relying on paper records.
•	Allows workshop staff to view customer profiles, upcoming service opportunities, appointment schedules and vehicle histories from one dashboard.
•	Introduces appropriate AI use through a maintenance knowledge base and simple advisory chatbot for common vehicle servicing questions.

# Customer Mobile App Requirements
•	Registration and login for customers.
•	Vehicle profile management including vehicle number, brand, model, year, mileage and ownership details.
•	Dashboard showing vehicle health score, next service due date, upcoming appointments and important alerts.
•	Digital service booklet to display past maintenance records, replaced parts, service dates, costs and remarks.
•	Appointment booking interface where customers can select service type, preferred date/time and add service notes.

Workshop Web Dashboard
•	Customer and vehicle management for service advisors and workshop managers.
•	Appointment calendar to review, approve, reschedule or cancel bookings.
•	Service record entry form for technicians or service advisors to update completed jobs.
•	Parts and wear-and-tear tracking screen for items such as engine oil, filters, brake pads, battery, tyres and fluids.
•	Dashboard analytics showing upcoming services, customers due for follow-up, monthly bookings and common service categories.

Maintenance Reminder and Wear-and-Tear Engine
•	Rule-based maintenance scheduling using mileage and time interval thresholds.
•	Traffic-light part condition indicators: Good, Monitor and Replace Soon.
•	Reminder generation for service intervals, expiring parts, battery age, tyre checks and fluid replacement.
•	Notification centre for upcoming service, appointment reminders, vehicle health alerts and promotional messages.

AI Maintenance Advisor
•	Chat-style assistant for general maintenance questions such as brake noise, battery lifespan, tyre wear or service intervals.
•	Knowledge base prepared from workshop-provided maintenance notes and common servicing FAQs.
•	Suggested responses should include practical next steps such as “book inspection”, “monitor condition”, or “replace soon”.
•	AI responses must include a disclaimer that the advice is not a replacement for a professional inspection.

Quotation and Basic Billing Module (Optional)
•	Workshop staff can generate simple service quotations by selecting labour items, parts and package options.
•	Customers can view quotation summaries from the app before confirming service.
•	The system can export a basic quotation or service summary as PDF for customer reference.

Customer Loyalty and Retention
•	Reward points for completed servicing, referrals or package sign-ups.
•	Simple promotions and reminders to encourage regular maintenance.
•	At-risk customer list for vehicles that have not returned after a recommended service period.

# Deliverables
1) Customer Mobile App Prototype
Login, vehicle profile, dashboard, service history, appointment booking, notifications and AI assistant.

2) Workshop Web Dashboard
Customer management, vehicle management, service records, appointment calendar, reminders and analytics.

3) Database and Backend API
Relational database, authentication, REST APIs and role-based access for customers and workshop users.

4) Maintenance Rules Engine
Mileage/time-based reminders and wear-and-tear status calculation for commonly replaced parts.

5) AI Maintenance Advisor
Chatbot prototype with curated maintenance knowledge base and safe advisory responses.

6) Testing and Documentation
Test plan, user guide, technical documentation, deployment notes and final presentation materials.
